# strangersh

A name derived from my dictionary by combining two words stranger and ssh. The project is designed to make you feel like you're chatting with a stranger using SSH through terminal rather than conventional websites. The bot acts like a human and we're supposed to waste out time chatting with it :p


## About:

I've used Bubble Tea, Bubbles and Lip Gloss for screen, text box and color designs. I've used nemotron-3.5-super 120b model to have quick response and its also not super demanding like GLM. Thinking has also been turned off to have faster output. This is hosted in Hack Club Nest and with bore.pub tunnel.

## How to use:

Connect to the project through SSH.
`ssh -p 32128 bore.pub`

Choose your username for the conversation.

You'll meet with random people with their own personalities and background. 

Be aware that if you turn out to be disrespectful or boring, they may skip you.

`Note: ctrl+n does next stranger` 


## Features: 

-> 10 strangers with their own personality and background.
-> Strangers leave if you're boring or rude.
-> "searching for a stranger..." between switches.
-> AI summary at the end about conversation and why it ended in narrated form.

## Idea:

I got this random idea out of nowhere. Few weeks ago me and my friends used a website in computer lab where we would chat with strangers from different place. This gave me an idea to build something similar.
