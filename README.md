# strangersh

A name derived from my dictionary by combining two words stranger and ssh. The project is designed to make you feel like you're chatting with a stranger using SSH through terminal rather than conventional websites.

A chatbot type project that tries to act like a human and we (the actual human) will knowingly waste our time chatting with a bot.

I've used nemotron 3 super 120b model to have quick response and its also not super demanding like GLM. Thinking has also been turned off to have faster output.

## How to use:

Connect to the project through SSH. 

You'll meet with random people with their own personalities and background. 

Be aware that if you turn out to be disrespectful or boring, they may skip you 

`Note: ctrl+n does next stranger` 


# Idea:

I got this random idea out of nowhere. Few weeks ago me and my friends used a website in computer lab where we would chat with strangers from different place. This gave me an idea to build something similar.
